###  AI LAB
A pointer-reactive mathematical environment representing the intersection of computation, geometry, and AI.

**How to interact:**
- **Move your cursor** across the screen to dynamically manipulate the radial glass structure.
- **Observe** the geometric layers twist and respond to your pointer's coordinates in real-time.

`COMPUTATION × GEOMETRY × AI`

###  ZERO GRAVITY
A physics-inspired 3D environment where objects orbit through a weightless digital space.

**How to interact:**
- **Drag or swipe** across the viewport to rotate the camera around the central void.
- **Hover** over the floating geometric primitives to see subtle interactive feedback.
